In my MASc, I developed a Monte Carlo, two-stage bottom-up model that learns facility-level maintenance policies and allocates a network budget to either maximize the state of good repair or minimize global warming potential (GWP).

Results from a network-level case study:

- maximizing network condition **raises expected GWP by 1–8%** over 20 years;
- shifting funds from reconstruction to rehabilitation **improves condition by up to 25% and cuts GWP by up to 7%**.

**Paper:** del Campo Yañez, J. M., Lee, M. M. J., & Swei, O. (2023). Sustainable infrastructure is a two-way street: Balancing environmental and condition performance goals. _Journal of Management in Engineering_, 39(3), 05023004. [doi:10.1061/JMENEA.MEENG-5225](https://doi.org/10.1061/JMENEA.MEENG-5225)
